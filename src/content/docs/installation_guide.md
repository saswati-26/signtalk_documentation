## Installation Guide:

This is the installation guide for the installation of **SignMate** mobile application. This contains the complete guide along with the respective screenshots for your convenience.

<br />
<br />

**Step 1:** Click on the installation link provided in the [Getting Started - Installation](/getting_started#installation) based on your OS type (Android or iOS)
![installation link](/src/assets/SignMate-removebg.png "Installation Link")

<br />

**Step 2:** After clicking on the link the app starts downloading. It will take some time depending upon your internet speed and will get downloaded.

<br />

**Step 3:** After the downloading is completed, now click on the `install` button and install the app.

<br />

**Step 4:** After installation click on `open` to open the app.

<br />

![link video](/src/assets/install_videos/VN20241211_124914.mp4)
![install video](/src/assets/install_videos/VN20241211_125055.mp4)
![open video](/src/assets/install_videos/VN20241211_125132.mp4)

<br />
Now, **SignMate** has successfully installed in your mobile phone.
